Candara
=======

Family: Candara
Style: Regular
Version: 0.90

Source: https://github.com/Adebayobenjamin/CandaraFont/blob/main/CandaraFont.ttf

The WOFF2 asset preserves the original font metadata. CSS requests bold italic; browsers synthesize the weight and slant when native Candara Bold Italic is unavailable.
